How this engine works

    
    
      
  • Ephemeris: Swiss Ephemeris C library (pyswisseph) with NASA JPL DE431 files — sub-arcsecond planetary precision.
    • 
      
  • Ayanamsa: Lahiri (Chitrapaksha) — the standard used by Indian calendars and most professional jyotish software.
    • 
      
  • Houses: Whole-Sign (Rasi) — the sign containing the Ascendant becomes House 1, continuing counter-clockwise.
    • 
      
  • Grahas: Surya, Chandra, Mangal, Budha, Guru, Shukra, Shani, Rahu (mean node) and Ketu (exactly 180° from Rahu).
    • 
      
  • Dasha: Vimshottari balance measured astronomically by finding the exact instant the Moon entered its natal nakshatra.
    •
